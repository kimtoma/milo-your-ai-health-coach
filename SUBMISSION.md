# Hackathon submission

**Team:** Forever on a DIET

**Project:** Milo — An Empathetic GLP-1 & Everyday Wellness Companion

**Project description:**

An empathetic AI companion powered by Gemini 4 Argon that helps people using GLP-1 treatments keep track of injections, weight, meals, movement and wellbeing. Milo combines warm, context-aware conversations with records that users review and save. A bilingual Material Design interface and an isolated 30-day demo turn those records into clear period reports with browser print-to-PDF and CSV export.
